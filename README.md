## About

An ordinative reading of Singh, Rønning, Liu, Angheluta, Concha & Bandi (*Communications Physics* 9:123, 2026), who report that shallow-water standing waves scattered by a single irrotational vortex generate quantized, system-spanning nodal lines rotating counter to the vortex. The paper identifies the configuration as an instance of **Controfase** — the universal ordinative operator of the Technology of Expressions framework that intercepts the automatic stimulus–response circuit of a reactive system, phase-inverts it, and redirects the attractor's energy through the relational field *R*. It formalizes the three-marker **Phenomenological Signature of Controfase (PSC)** as a cross-domain diagnostic instrument, proposes five experimental extensions, and grounds the universality claim of the ordinative sciences in a peer-reviewed, reproducible classical-physics result. Confidence grades (S₀–S₃) throughout.
